Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

Sichuan Parabola Technology Co., Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the WhatUpBaby mobile application (the "App") for iOS. We are committed to protecting the privacy of your family. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data privacy.

1. Complete Offline & On-Device Processing (No Collection)

  • No Account Creation: The App does not require or support user registration or login.
  • On-Device Computation: All video, audio, and biophysical data processing—including Eulerian Video Magnification (rPPG) and facial landmark analysis via Google MediaPipe—occurs 100% locally on your iOS device.
  • Zero Transmission: No camera feeds, facial meshes, biometric arrays, or personal identification data are ever transmitted to our servers, third parties, or cloud storage.

2. Local Storage Only

Any diagnostics, generated PDFs, or exported medical reports are stored strictly within the App's secure local sandbox or your device’s local file system. If you delete the App, all locally saved data will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered by us.

3. iOS System Permissions

To deliver the core single-purpose utility, the App requires explicit access to:

  • Camera: To scan sub-visual skin color shifts and facial muscle tension.
  • Microphone: To isolate and analyze respiratory sound waves.
  • Files/Storage: To export structural PDF reports locally.

These permissions are utilized strictly for real-time offline processing.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed as a tool for parents/guardians of infants and children aged 0-6. Because all processing is restricted to the local hardware interface and no data is gathered, we do not knowingly or physically collect any Personal Information from children.
